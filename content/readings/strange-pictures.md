## Reflections & Notes

This book is thrilling. It turns the reader into an investigator giving you biased views of unsettling hand drawn pictures and clues. Slowly revealing new aspects as the story progresses. I love each of the short stories in this book. It's not gory, there aren't jump scares. It's chilling, with a slow spreading, creeping feeling of something wrong. If you grew up reading _Goosebumps_ as a kid or enjoy Stephen King you'll probably love this book!
