I love this song. The changes in tempo, the tone color it has a unique impressionist evocation to it. The whole album _Introspection_ (recorded in 1972) is a fanstic listen and the album art is pretty epic. If you haven't heard it before I highly recommend. Another one of my favorites of his is [Concerto for Guitar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iYgTrNOsMBI&t=778s).
