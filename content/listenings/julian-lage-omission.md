This is one of my current favorite acoustic pieces. The rhythmic and melodic drone in the beginning is infectious. Julian is brilliant and such an emmotive/expressive musician. The guitar
to him certainly seems like an extension of his voice and storytelling ability. I love how he phrases things, leaving space
while also throwing in notes or lines that have you going like _what just happened_ ... and somehow it always makes sense.
