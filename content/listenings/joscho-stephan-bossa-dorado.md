Joscho is a beast! Super percise playing with incredible speed and clarity. This tune was one of my intros to him and his trio, Sven his rhythm guitarist is a legend. There's a few fun licks smoothly incorporated into this tune (Paint it Black & Pink Panther for starters). Grooving minor vamps, with fantastic solos and a very catchy and melodic chorus line. Highly recommend a listen.
