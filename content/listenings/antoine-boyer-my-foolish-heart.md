Antoine Boyer is most certainly my favorite guitarist. He has incredible chops, but always seems to play for the song itself. There are no blistering runs for the sake of it - every note means something. I love all his originals, but this interpretation of _"My Foolish Heart_" is all time. Expressive, tender, rich, with moments of space and freedom. It is one of those performances where you can feel the literal meaning of the song's title. Hope you enjoy.
