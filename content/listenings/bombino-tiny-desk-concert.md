Bombino is such a phenomenal guitarist. His electric Tuareg desert blues grooves are completely hypnotic and full of infectious energy. I love coming back to this Tiny Desk concert—the rhythm section locks in so tight. The song _"Imuhar"_ in particular always has me grooving no matter where or what state I'm in.
