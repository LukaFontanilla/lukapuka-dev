This has been one of my favorite live performances to listen to over the years. I find myself going back to it often and I always end up closing my eyes, getting lost in the music with a heavy sway (especially to those pockets in My All, if you know you know!). I think this was the first place I had heard Melanie play and was immediately captivated by her interpretation of Jimi Hendrix's "Little Wing" and Mariah Carey's "My All". The tone, the feel, the groove insatiable stuff.

Shoutout Melanie Faye, you're awesome and an inspiration.
